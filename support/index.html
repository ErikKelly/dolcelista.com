Support

Questions, a bug, or an idea? Email support@dolcelista.com and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

I didn't get my sign-in code.
Check your spam or junk folder for an email from hello@dolcelista.com. Codes expire after a while, so tap Send a new code if it's been more than a few minutes.

How do I join a friend's list?
Ask the list's owner for its code. In the app, tap Join at the top of the Lists screen and enter it. Codes work for 7 days.

How do I invite people to my list?
Open the list, tap ··· → Invite people, then Share code. Only the list's owner can invite.

How do I leave a list, or remove someone?
Open the list and tap the member avatars. Members can leave; the owner can remove people.

How do I delete my account?
In the app, go to Profile → Delete account. This removes your profile, the lists you own and your ratings, reviews and comments. See the privacy policy for details.
